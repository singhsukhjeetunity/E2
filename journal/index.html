Backup & restore

Back up after imports and before updating the app. Your journal is stored separately from E2 code.

Download complete backup
Restore a saved journal

Restoring replaces the current journal. A safety backup is saved first, and folder imports are paused for review.

  1. Keep the EA independent

    Your standalone EA runs in MT5 as before. Do not install or start the old controller or synthetic runner. This journal cannot submit, approve, modify or close a trade.

  2. Add your accounts

    Accounts & allocations → add each eval, funded, demo or backtest dataset. Enter the balance immediately before the history you will import, currency and reporting clock.

  3. Export and import

    Enable InpCsvExportEnabled in E2 at a safe planned restart. Current E2 writes reports when the EA shuts down or a backtest finishes. Reports are in MT5’s Common Files → E2 → Reports. Upload the _T.csv; add _S.csv for signal outcomes.

  4. Let the app recognise the CSV

    Select its account, preview, then confirm. No column mapping or file renaming is needed. Repeated uploads are safe. Other strategies can use the downloadable E2_JOURNAL_V1 template.

  5. Optional folder automation

    Assign an account-specific folder in Import centre. Leave the app running; matching stable exports are read automatically. This does not make the EA export continuously and does not pull MT5 history.

  6. Review, then act in MT5

    Compare years and strategy variants. Record grades, reference risk, payouts and fees. Any risk change or strategy shutdown is your decision, applied directly in the EA.

What the numbers mean

Net P&L includes reported commission, swap and fees. Win rate counts net-positive closed trades. Expectancy is average net R using each trade’s actual initial cash risk. Profit factor is positive net results divided by absolute negative net results.

Drawdown uses cumulative closed-trade P&L from zero, combining exits at the same timestamp. It is not broker equity drawdown or a prop-rule compliance check. No claim of live account status or complete history is made.

Account balances are reconstructed from your starting balance, imported trades and balance transfers. Dates are kept exactly in the report clock—no guessed UTC conversion. Do not mix reports with different clocks in one dataset.

Use a new backtest dataset for each test run, and keep backtests out of real-capital totals. Review flagged imports. Pausing a strategy here only changes a label.
